## Always-on rules
- Protect tenant isolation, evidence provenance, human control and cost limits.
- Never fabricate data, passing checks, citations, prices, ROI or compliance.
- Consequential scoring is deterministic and versioned.
- No individual adverse employment decisions.
- Prefer typed contracts, tests, adapters and reversible changes.
- Activate relevant trusted skills just in time and audit their use.
- Stop at phase checkpoints for approval.
