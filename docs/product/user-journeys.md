# FuturaSculptAI: User Journeys & Persona Workflows

FuturaSculptAI serves seven primary enterprise stakeholders through dedicated perspectives in a unified cockpit.

## 1. Executive & Board Director (CEO / Board)
- **Goal:** Understand enterprise exposure to emerging technology, identify strategic value pools, and evaluate investment risk across multi-year horizons.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Accesses the **Executive Cockpit**.
  2. Inspects the **Scenario Matrix** (Conservative vs. Accelerated vs. Disruptive vs. Human-First).
  3. Reviews enterprise-wide **Value Pools** ($ EBITDA expansion vs. transition cost).
  4. Drills into strategic risks and capital allocation requirements.
  5. Exports a cryptographically signed **Board Transformation Dossier**.

## 2. Chief Operating Officer & Transformation Leader (COO)
- **Goal:** Redesign end-to-end operational workflows, optimize human-agent task allocation, and manage organizational transition.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Opens the **Task Sculptor** and views the current process flow (e.g., Food Quality Assurance).
  2. Evaluates task classification: `Retain`, `Assist`, `Augment`, `Automate`, `Redesign`, `Retire`.
  3. Inspects agent augmentation bottlenecks and human handoffs.
  4. Approves proposed operating model changes under A1/A2 governance.
  5. Tracks rollout milestones in the **Outcome Ledger**.

## 3. Chief Information Officer / Chief Technology Officer (CIO / CTO)
- **Goal:** Evaluate system dependencies, govern agent integrations, review cloud architecture blueprints, and monitor FinOps budgets.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Explores the **Architecture View** inside the **Opportunity Forge**.
  2. Inspects generated **Google ADK multi-agent specifications** and MCP tool bindings.
  3. Reviews the **FinOps Telemetry**: model gateway routing rules, context caching efficiency, and cost per transformation case.
  4. Validates API contracts, BigQuery schema projections, and Pub/Sub event schemas.

## 4. Chief Human Resources Officer & Workforce Leader (CHRO)
- **Goal:** Protect workforce vitality, minimize organizational friction, build career resilience, and guide proactive skilling.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Monitors aggregate workforce metrics: **Role Resilience Index (RRI)** and **Workforce Adaptability Index (WAI)**.
  2. Ensures compliance with **Responsible Workforce Red Lines** (cohort thresholds >= 10, no individual adverse profiling).
  3. Evaluates emerging role demand curves (e.g., Vision Pipeline Supervisor, AI Ethics Steward).
  4. Commissions personalized learning and mentoring transition pathways.

## 5. Chief Information Security Officer & Chief Compliance Officer (CISO)
- **Goal:** Enforce Google SAIF principles, verify privacy and PII redaction, prevent prompt injection, and validate audit logs.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Opens the **TrustBoard**.
  2. Reviews the 6 Well-Architected pillar scores and SAIF control matrix.
  3. Inspects **Model Armor** telemetry and Cloud DLP de-identification logs.
  4. Approves or blocks A3 reversible actions based on least-privilege service account permissions.
  5. Audits the tamper-evident **Evidence Ledger** and action approvals.

## 6. Frontline Employee / Knowledge Worker
- **Goal:** Understand how one's role is evolving, discover adjacent career opportunities, and access personalized, consent-based upskilling.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Logs into the **Personalized Pathways** portal under explicit personal consent.
  2. Explores the role's augmentation outlook without fear-based survival framing.
  3. Identifies transferable core competencies and target adjacent roles.
  4. Enrolls in curated micro-learning pathways, mentor connections, and internal sandboxes.

## 7. Investor & Due Diligence Partner (PE / VC / M&A)
- **Goal:** Conduct rapid, self-service operational due diligence on target portfolio companies and assess transformation upside.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Accesses the **Investor Lens** sandbox.
  2. Uploads redacted target materials (strategy deck, process SOP, org structure, tech stack).
  3. Obtains an automated **Diligence Report** in < 15 minutes:
     - 3-Year EBITDA unlock potential.
     - Agentic maturity and technical debt score.
     - Talent transition risk and human opportunity score.
     - Defensible competitive moat analysis.
  4. Adjusts macroeconomic and wage assumptions dynamically to model sensitivity.
