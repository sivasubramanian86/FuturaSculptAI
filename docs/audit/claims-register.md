## 1. Claims Classification

Every public claim across README, ARCHITECTURE.md, UI dashboards, and pitch decks is mapped to one of:
- **LIVE:** Implemented in codebase, covered by automated tests, deployed to Google Cloud, and verified live.
- **SIMULATED:** Deterministic demonstration algorithm; clearly labelled as synthetic simulation in UI.
- **PLANNED:** Architectural roadmap only; not claimed as currently operational.
- **CORRECTED:** Previously inflated claim retracted or reformulated to reflect technical reality.
