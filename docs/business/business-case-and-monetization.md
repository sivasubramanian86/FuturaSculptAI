## 2. Customer Segmentation: Will Startups to Big Companies Pay?

### 2.1 Startups & High-Growth Scale-Ups (50 - 500 Employees)
- **Pain Point:** Lack HR strategy teams or budgets for external consulting; need to scale operational capacity without tripling headcount.
- **Value Proposition:** Rapid task sculpting, identifying where AI agents eliminate repetitive backlog, establishing career ladders for technical staff.
- **Willingness to Pay:** **$1,500 - $3,500 / month ($18,000 - $42,000 ARR)**.
- **Decision Maker:** Chief Operating Officer / VP of People.

### 2.2 Mid-Market Enterprises (500 - 5,000 Employees)
- **Pain Point:** Introducing robotics and AI tools causes employee anxiety, union friction, and uneven adoption across plants.
- **Value Proposition:** WorkTwin Fabric RoleTwins simulate the operational impact of cobots/vision cameras before hardware purchase; provides deterministic Role Resilience Index (RRI) and wage-protection covenants.
- **Willingness to Pay:** **$10,000 - $25,000 / month ($120,000 - $300,000 ARR)**.
- **Decision Maker:** VP of Operations / Chief Human Resources Officer.

### 2.3 Global Industrial Conglomerates (5,000 - 100,000+ Employees)
- **Pain Point:** Enormous regulatory exposure under the **EU AI Act**, **OSHA ergonomics rules**, and collective bargaining agreements; multi-million dollar penalties for unlawful worker profiling or unauthorized AI deployment.
- **Value Proposition:** The Future Boardroom multi-agent simulation generates auditable consensus across CEO, CTO, CFO, CHRO, and CISO personas; EvidenceEnvelope cryptographic hashing guarantees tamper-evident compliance.
- **Willingness to Pay:** **$250,000 - $1,500,000+ Annual Contract Value (ACV)**.
- **Decision Maker:** Board of Directors / Chief Executive Officer / Chief Legal & Compliance Officer.
