# FuturaSculptAI: Product Charter

## 1. Vision & Identity

- **Product Name:** FuturaSculptAI
- **Tagline:** Predict change. Sculpt work. Create opportunity.
- **Category:** Autonomous, evidence-grounded Work Transformation Operating System.
- **Hackathon Track:** AI Builder Cup 2026 — Track: Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity ([aibuildercup.com](https://aibuildercup.com/)).

## 2. Core Problem Statement

Enterprises face accelerated shifts driven by AI agents, spatial computing, robotics, and regulatory dynamics. Existing enterprise talent platforms (Fuel50, Prismforce, Eightfold, Workday Skills Cloud) are tethered to the traditional HR application layer: they map resumes to job descriptions, catalogue static skills, or provide basic automation exposure metrics. They do not connect boardroom strategy, end-to-end operational processes, technical system dependencies, AI agent work allocation, architectural security boundaries, and unit economics into an executable transformation operating model.

When enterprises attempt transformation, they suffer from:
1. **Disconnected Execution:** Strategy decks are divorced from task-level workflows and IT architectures.
2. **Fear-Driven Automation:** Binary automation metrics lead to employee panic and attrition rather than proactive skill transition.
3. **Ungoverned Agent Deployment:** Ad-hoc agent adoption lacks SAIF/Well-Architected controls, leading to prompt injection, cost overruns, and data leakage.
4. **Unverifiable ROI:** Business cases rely on speculative, ungrounded spreadsheets rather than temporal baseline-to-realized ledgers.

## 3. Product Mission & Value Proposition

FuturaSculptAI delivers a **closed-loop Work Transformation Operating System** that:
- **Senses** dated external signals, technology maturity curves, regulatory changes, and internal operational evidence.
- **Models** a temporal **Work Transformation Graph** connecting outcomes, capabilities, processes, tasks, roles, skills, systems, data, agents, controls, and costs.
- **Simulates** four distinct future scenario classes (Conservative, Accelerated, Disruptive, Human-First) with explicit counterfactuals and confidence intervals.
- **Sculpts** future operating models, balancing human augmentation and autonomous agent workflows, creating emerging roles and personalized transition pathways.
- **Governs** proposals using Google SAIF, Well-Architected frameworks, and responsible workforce red lines.
- **Executes** approved plans into deployable Google ADK agent specifications, integration contracts, and backlogs.
- **Measures & Learns** by continuously comparing forecast baselines against actual outcomes in an immutable Outcome Ledger.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Objective | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to Value | Time-to-First-Transformation-Case (Sandbox) | < 15 minutes |
| Evidence Lineage | Claim-to-Evidence Groundedness Rate | 100% of factual assertions |
| Action Governance | Red-line Breach or Unauthorized A3/A4 Actions | 0 |
| Workforce Optimism | Human Opportunity vs. Automation Ratio | >= 1.5x new roles/augmentation paths |
| Architecture Quality | Well-Architected & SAIF Compliance Score | >= 90/100 across all pillars |
