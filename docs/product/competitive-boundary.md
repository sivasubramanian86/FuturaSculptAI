## 2. Explicit Anti-Goals: What We Do NOT Build

To maintain a defensible competitive moat and avoid commoditization, FuturaSculptAI explicitly excludes:

1. **Generic Skills Inventory / Resume Parser:** We do not build resume uploaders that parse bullet points into static skills tags.
2. **Basic Job Board / Internal Marketplace:** We do not build another UI for employees to browse open internal job postings.
3. **Standard HR Chatbot:** We do not build a conversational wrapper over employee handbooks or HR benefits documents.
4. **Binary Automation Fear Scores:** We do not calculate sensationalist "75% of your job will be replaced by AI" metrics.
5. **AI-Generated Generic Job Descriptions:** We do not generate boilerplates for job postings without system, task, and architectural linkages.
6. **Isolated ROI Calculators:** We do not present static spreadsheet formulas with fabricated assumptions; all ROI models require verifiable baselines and counterfactuals.
